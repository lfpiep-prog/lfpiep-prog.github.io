The Hook Room Pipeline

A private tool run by Lenni (github.com/lfpiep-prog). It uploads short videos to the operator's own YouTube channels (The Hook Room and Storm Decoded) and reads their statistics to see which videos perform well.

The tool runs only on the operator's own computer. It is not offered to anyone else, and no other people's accounts or data are accessed.
